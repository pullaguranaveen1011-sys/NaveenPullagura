OVERVIEW & SCOPE
01 Product Shift

Originally built as a 360-degree feedback module tucked inside NuVeda's core LMS (CALF), leadership recognized strong enterprise demand and decided to spin it off into an autonomous commercial product. However, the initial engineering extraction was disjointed: missing onboarding, lacking enterprise white-labeling, and saddled with complex LMS navigation paths. Partnering closely with NuVeda leadership and engineering, I led the end-to-end UX redesign—establishing the standalone information architecture, 3-step white-label onboarding, and production design system.

My Screen-by-Screen Forensic Audit in Figma

Because this was my very first project at NuVeda, I took screenshots of every single existing legacy screen and user flow. In Figma, I mapped each screen sequentially against the user's mental model. This forensic audit uncovered critical structural flaws: buried action buttons, unformatted data tables, and an absence of loading states that caused duplicate form submissions.

01 / ONBOARDING EXPERIENCE

Getting started should be effortless

Every enterprise tool needs to establish trust in the first 3 minutes. I designed an intuitive 3-step onboarding sequence.

STEP 01

Workspace URL & Subdomain

Admins define their custom workspace URL (e.g., company.nu360.com). The interface provides real-time validation to verify domain availability immediately, establishing organizational boundaries.

STEP 02

Organization Profile & White-Labeling

Enterprise clients need their employees to trust the survey source. Admins upload their logo and select primary brand colors with a dynamic live preview of the rater evaluation interface.

STEP 03

Language Settings & Verification

Configuring multi-lingual support for global enterprise workforces and establishing two-factor email verification to protect sensitive feedback data.

The Post-Onboarding Command Center: After completing the 3-step setup, administrators arrive at a unified dashboard displaying active 360 feedback cycles, rater completion rates, and an always-accessible AI credit balance.

Always-accessible wallet displaying remaining credits, token burn rate, and 1-click tier upgrade packs without navigating away from active surveys.

AUDIT

Credit History & Transaction Log

Complete historical ledger of purchased credit batches, invoice receipts, and transparent breakdowns of which survey cycles consumed credits.

KEY AI UX DECISION

Pre-Flight Cost Transparency

Before any AI generation starts, the UI surfaces a clear confirmation: "Generating this 360 framework will consume 5 credits. Your balance: 42 credits." This simple confirmation eliminated billing anxiety and prevented accidental token waste.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

A senior design reflection on NuVeda 360

Lessons and systems thinking principles distilled from leading this enterprise redesign

01

Architect for Autonomy, Not Just Independence

You cannot simply rip a feature out of a monolith LMS and slap a new logo on it. Standalone products require their own authentication, workspace navigation, multi-tenant billing, and onboarding mental models.

02

Economic Clarity Drives AI Adoption

When introducing generative AI into enterprise B2B SaaS, the interaction design must make token costs transparent. Surfacing credit previews before generation gave HR admins complete peace of mind.

03

Speed to Value Outranks Feature Quantity

By shrinking onboarding down to 3 minutes and survey creation from 4 hours to 12 minutes, we delivered immediate commercial ROI without cluttering the interface with unnecessary bells and whistles.
