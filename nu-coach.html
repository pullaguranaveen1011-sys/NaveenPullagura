The Genesis

From Raw AI Prototype to Enterprise Product

Product Manager Deepak built an initial prototype on Lovable AI to test the concept. While it proved the core hypothesis, the experience had significant UI debt, zero gamification, and fragmented flows.

Design Assessment The Gap to Close

The initial AI prototype built by PM Deepak validated that an LLM could evaluate workplace interactions. However, it lacked visual hierarchy, predictable design tokens, and retention mechanisms. Collaborating closely with Deepak on product requirements and edge cases, I led the complete design transformation: establishing a scalable design system, introducing habit-building streaks, and making the AI interactions intuitive, transparent, and enterprise-ready.

Component Debt

Unstandardized fonts, inconsistent paddings, and raw AI input elements.

No Retention Mechanics

Lacked streaks, leaderboards, or rewards to bring learners back.

Unguided AI Feedback

Interactions felt like a cold test rather than supportive executive coaching.

Strategic Architecture

Key Design Decisions That Redefined NU Coach

Transforming rapid AI code into an enterprise product requires systems thinking. Here are the 4 core design bets I made.

01 / Habit Architecture

Gamification & Leaderboards

Replaced sporadic training with sticky daily routines: weekly simulation streaks, XP multipliers, peer leaderboards, and immediate post-incident reward feedback.

02 / Empathic Audio UX

Conversational AI Coach

Replaced intimidating blank prompts with live audio waveforms, real-time speech transcription, dynamic sentiment indicators, and supportive executive coaching.

03 / Systems Logic

Branching Scenario Engine

Architected realistic crisis cockpits where learner choices branch into distinct consequences under SLA countdown pressure, measuring composure and leadership judgment.

04 / Enterprise Rigor

Scalable Design Tokens

Built a production Figma token architecture supporting OLED dark and light modes, strict non-deterministic AI state matrices (streaming, low confidence, latency), and 48px touch ergonomics.
