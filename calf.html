Honest Reality Check

The legacy product was in severe visual disarray when I took over design ownership. There was no design system in place, and erratic paddings, mismatched typography, and ad-hoc icons plagued every view. Rather than rushing an uncontrolled rewrite, I executed a disciplined two-phase strategy: first stabilize foundational hierarchy and spacing, then architect the 2.0 multi-tenant overhaul.

Enterprise FinOps & Governance

Storage Quotas, Email Credits & AI Consumption Hub

How an unexpected surge in cloud storage and email infrastructure expenses triggered a strategic product redesign — turning an unmonitored operational cost leak into an intuitive, self-serve resource management and monetization engine.

THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Uncapped Cloud Storage & Infrastructure Cost Spikes

As CALF 2.0 scaled across enterprise clients, platform usage surged dramatically. Corporate training administrators and facilitators were uploading gigabytes of uncompressed high-definition video lessons, SCORM packages, and media assets, while triggering hundreds of thousands of automated learner notification emails.

Because legacy CALF had zero hard storage quotas or email delivery caps, NuVeda was hit with massive cloud infrastructure bills (AWS S3 storage and transactional email delivery) that the company had to absorb and pay out-of-pocket rather than billing back to clients.

Following an update to our commercial Terms & Conditions (T&C), I was tasked with designing a transparent, self-serve Credit & Storage Management Hub that gives enterprise clients real-time visibility into their consumption, allows them to clean up obsolete assets, and provides self-serve add-on purchasing for email credits and Gen-AI blocks.

01

Cloud Storage Meter & Asset Cleanup

Visual capacity gauges showing real-time GB/TB usage against subscription plan limits. Empowered administrators to audit file categories (videos, SCORMs, documents) and directly delete orphan files to reclaim allocated storage.

02

Email Notification Quota & Top-Ups

Granular tracking of automated transactional emails and learner campaigns. Proactive threshold warnings (80% / 90%) prevent bounced reminder emails, with instant self-serve purchasing of add-on email credit packs.

03

AI Credit Blocks & Authoring Balance

Dedicated balance meters for Generative AI authoring features (AI Course Creator, automated quiz generator, and lesson summarizers), allowing teams to buy supplementary credit blocks on-demand.

04

Ambient Menubar Quick-Access Widget

A compact, non-intrusive status indicator situated persistently at the bottom of the main navigation menubar, providing at-a-glance capacity health and a one-click gateway to the management hub.
