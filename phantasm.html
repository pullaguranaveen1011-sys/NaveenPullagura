US Legal State Markets

The US legal cannabis and vape industry operates under strict state regulations: mandatory 21+ age verification, seed-to-sale inventory compliance, wholesale excise taxes, and warehouse logistics. However, licensed distributors and warehouse operators were constrained by 10-year-old legacy software that couldn't sync with consumer storefronts.

At Phantasm Solutions, our mission was to build Smokewana from scratch — an integrated white-label ecosystem delivering a B2B Warehouse Management System (WMS), responsive e-commerce web storefront, customer mobile apps (iOS & Android), and a zero-code layout customizer that requires zero developer intervention.

THE CHALLENGE & RESEARCH

Auditing legacy tools & identifying the gaps

We audited existing enterprise warehouse tools and uncovered major operational bottlenecks holding back US distributors.

01
BOTTLENECK 01

Siloed Inventory & Stockouts

Legacy software kept warehouse counts on offline servers, resulting in frequent stockouts, double-booking, and slow phone verifications. We designed real-time inventory sync across facilities and digital storefronts.

02
BOTTLENECK 02

Rigid Layouts & Dev Costs

Updating a promotional banner or category required hiring developers. We built a modular zero-code customizer so business owners can change headers, footers, and brand colors in minutes.

03
BOTTLENECK 03

Manual Wholesale Tier Pricing

Distributors negotiated bulk prices via offline spreadsheets. We designed an automated setup tool that applies volume case discounts and state excise taxes dynamically upon login.

04
BOTTLENECK 04

Frictionless 21+ Age Compliance

Blunt overlay gates caused 40%+ bounce rates. We designed a compliant age verification pattern that stores consent securely across sessions without blocking product browsing.

THE CORE PRODUCT

Smokewana: Multi-Channel B2B WMS Platform

A unified operating system built from scratch to power wholesale warehouse operations, delivery logistics, and storefront customization.

01

Zero-Code Storefront Customizer

A visual layout builder empowering non-technical operators to customize navigation bars, headers, footers, promo carousels, and brand colors without writing a single line of code.

02

B2B Warehouse & Inventory Hub

Centralized multi-facility stock management: live inventory counts, barcode scanner intake, batch/lot tracking for seed-to-sale compliance, and automated low-stock PO triggers.

03

Fleet Delivery Dispatch & Logistics

Complete last-mile delivery infrastructure: driver route assignments, state compliance manifest checklists, digital proof-of-delivery signatures, and real-time customer tracking.

04

Wholesale Tier Pricing & Setup Tool

A comprehensive setup module for configuring legal licenses, warehouse zones, dynamic bulk case discounts (1-10, 11-50, 50+ cases), state tax rules, and instant B2B invoicing.

SENIOR DESIGN REFLECTION

What designing for regulated US commerce taught me

Designing Smokewana and the Phantasm ecosystem reinforced a fundamental product design truth: the best UX work happens where extreme operational complexity meets non-technical human reality.

Warehouse workers need ruthless visual clarity, high-contrast status feedback, and zero-latency barcode scanning while packing bulk orders in busy facilities. Similarly, dispensary owners shouldn't have to hire engineers just to update a promotional banner or store hours. Collaborating closely with PM Sainath, UI Designer Prakash, and our graphic designer taught me how to operate as a high-velocity product design engine — synthesizing ambiguous business inputs into defensible wireframe architectures, co-designing polished UI screens, and delivering measurable enterprise value.
